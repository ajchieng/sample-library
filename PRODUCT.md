# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Music producers, sound designers, and sample-library-heavy creators who want a polished desktop tool for browsing, tagging, auditioning, and maintaining local audio samples. The primary context is a production session where speed, trust, and low-friction previewing matter more than marketing flourish.

## Product Purpose

Sample Tracker is a local-first desktop sample library. It stores file paths and metadata in SQLite, lets users import samples from disk without moving or uploading them, and supports search, filters, tagging, editing, preview playback, missing-file detection, relinking, and Finder reveal. Success means users can quickly find the right sound, trust that their files remain untouched, and keep library metadata organized without leaving the studio workflow.

## Brand Personality

Clean, modern, focused. The app should feel like a polished tool other producers could download and trust, while staying quiet enough to disappear into the work.

## Anti-references

Do not make the interface feel like a generic SaaS dashboard. Avoid marketing-site chrome, decorative metric cards, heavy corporate admin styling, flashy DJ/performance visuals, dark neon producer cliches, and Ableton-clone mimicry.

## Design Principles

- Keep the sample workflow primary: search, filter, select, audition, and edit should remain immediately available.
- Preserve local-first trust: file safety and missing-file states should be clear without becoming alarmist.
- Use modern restraint: clean surfaces, purposeful hierarchy, and consistent controls over decorative styling.
- Optimize for scanning: dense library data should be readable at a glance, with type, BPM, key, tags, missing state, and favorite state easy to compare.
- Let audio feel central: the player and waveform should feel integrated with the library, not bolted on.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Maintain keyboard navigation for the sample list and playback, visible focus states for interactive elements, clear labels for icon buttons, reduced-motion-safe transitions, and non-color-only indicators for missing files and selected states.
